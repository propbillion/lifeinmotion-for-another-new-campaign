About The Arena Pimpri.

The Arena Pimpri is Pune's first sports township, developed by Mahalaxmi Group in partnership with Kohinoor Group and Agarwal Sukhwani Associates - 11 towers of 2, 3 & 4 BHK homes built around an 80,000 sq ft sports and recreation ecosystem in the heart of Pimpri.

The sports ecosystem is operated by Ileseum Sports Management, with experience across 126 clubs nationally - bringing trained coaches, structured programs and professional upkeep to football turf, tennis, padel, pickleball, swimming and the full-floor Elysium Clubhouse.
